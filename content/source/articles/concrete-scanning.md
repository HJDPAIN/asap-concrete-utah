Concrete Scanning
July 12, 2020|Concrete Scanning

       We recently added concrete scanning to our services. This piece of equipment allows us to take a look inside and under the concrete. This scanner can detect conduit, pipe, post-tension cables, electrical wire, concrete thickness, wire mesh, and rebar!  It is important to know what is under the concrete to make your project go smoothly. Scanning concrete can save you money and help you plan your project. Cutting through unexpected conduit can add an extra expense that you might not have had in the bid for your project. It is also important to know what structural reinforcement you have in your concrete for remodels to ensure that the structure is stable.
