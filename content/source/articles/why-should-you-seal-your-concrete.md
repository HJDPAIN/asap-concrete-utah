Does Your Concrete Need To Be Sealed?
January 10, 2023|Concrete Sealer

      Sealing concrete is an important step to keep your driveways, sidewalks, and concrete pads in great shape.  If neglected, your concrete can quickly lose its finished surface.  When the surface of your concrete starts to chip up it is called "spalling". This can be caused by a few things. First, is road salts. The salts used to remove ice and snow have a damaging effect on your concrete. The chemicals in the salts get into the surface of your concrete and loosen the bonds of the concrete and weaken it. It is recommended to not put salts on your driveway for at least the first 3 years after it is poured.

       Second, is freeze/thaw cycles. Winters in Utah can be brutal and the temperatures can be unpredictable. Concrete is porous so water can seep into the surface of the concrete. When water gets into the surface of your concrete and freezes, the water expands and puts pressure on the concrete causing it to chip up. The combination of freeze/thaw cycles and road salts can be a nightmare for your concrete. 

       Example of what spalling or chipped concrete looks like. 




So how do you prevent your concrete from spalling? 

        The answer is simple. Avoid road salts, and apply a concrete sealer! There are many types of sealers that can be used based on how old the concrete is, or the type of finish that was done on it. For most outdoor concrete, a penetrating concrete sealer is best. This is what we mainly use on our customer's driveways. A penetrating sealer seeps into the surface of your concrete and creates a protective barrier to repel water, oil, road salts, and more. This sealer makes your driveway easier to clean if you happen to spill oil or other substances onto it. 

     The sealer we use does not change the color, texture, or sheen of your concrete.  Since this sealer repels water is prevents the freeze/thaw cycles by keeping the water on the surface of the concrete instead of it seeping in. We use a power washer to clean your driveway before we apply the sealer to ensure that it works properly. 

  Keeping your concrete looking great can be so simple but it is often neglected. A great looking driveway always adds curb appeal to your home or business.
