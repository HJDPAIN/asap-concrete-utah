*In-Floor Heating Before Concrete Is Poured In Basement*

In-Floor Heating can be a great addition when building a new home. These tubes have heated water running through them to keep your floors nice and warm.

A problem you don't want is to have the lines penetrated. If one of these lines get penetrated, you are in for a very expensive and difficult repair.

If you need to drill or anchor into concrete with in-floor heating underneath, it's best to know where these lines are and to stay as far away as possible from them.

Give us a call before you drill so we can help you locate these lines with our concrete scanner. The scanner will also show the thickness of the concrete so you know how deep you can drill before getting to the lines.
