## What it is

Concrete demolition is messy, loud and heavy. We saw-cut the edges first so what stays is clean, then break out the concrete with the right tool, from a hand breaker to a skid steer with a hydraulic hammer, and haul it off.

## When you need it

- Replacing a driveway, sidewalk or patio
- Opening a slab so plumbers can run new pipe underneath
- Removing footings, stem walls or old equipment pads
- Clearing a site for new construction

## What we need to quote

Rough square footage, thickness, access for equipment, and whether you want it hauled away. Photos help.
