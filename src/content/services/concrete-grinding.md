## What it is

Concrete grinding uses diamond grinding equipment to take down high spots, lipped joints and rough surfaces on concrete.

## When you need it

- Trip hazards on sidewalks and walkways
- Uneven joints between slabs
- Smoothing a floor before new flooring or coatings

## What we need to quote

How many spots, roughly how high the lip is, and where the job is. Photos are the fastest way to get an accurate number.
