## What it is

Our concrete scanner lets us look inside and under the concrete before anyone cuts. It detects conduit, pipe, post-tension cables, electrical wire, wire mesh and rebar, and shows the slab thickness.

## When you need it

- Before core drilling or sawing any slab you did not pour yourself
- Floors with in-floor heating, where one hit means an expensive repair
- Remodels, where you need to know what reinforcement is in the structure
- Anchoring equipment or racking into an existing floor

## What we need to quote

Job location, the area to scan, and what you plan to do afterward (drill, cut, anchor). We can scan and cut in one visit.
