## What it is

A penetrating sealer soaks into the surface of the concrete and creates a barrier that repels water, oil and road salt. We power-wash the concrete first so the sealer bonds properly.

## Why it matters in Utah

Winters here are hard on concrete. Water gets into the pores, freezes, expands and chips the surface. Road salts make it worse. Sealed concrete keeps the water on top instead of inside, which stops spalling and makes spills easier to clean.

## What we need to quote

Square footage, the age of the concrete, and whether it has ever been sealed. A photo of the current surface helps.
