## What it is

Core drilling uses a diamond-tipped hollow bit to cut a precise round hole through concrete, block or brick. The hole is clean and the right size the first time.

## When you need it

- Plumbing and drain lines through slabs and foundations
- Electrical conduit and HVAC penetrations
- Dryer and furnace vents, sump pits, floor drains
- Anchor bolts, railing posts and concrete test samples

## What we need to quote

Hole diameter, how many holes, concrete thickness and the job address. If the hole is in a floor with in-floor heat or post-tension cables, scan first.
