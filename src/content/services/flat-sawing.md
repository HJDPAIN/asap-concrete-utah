## What it is

Flat sawing (also called slab sawing) uses a walk-behind saw with a diamond blade to make straight cuts in horizontal concrete: floors, slabs, driveways, sidewalks, parking lots and roads.

## When you need it

- Trenches through a slab so plumbers or electricians can run new lines
- Removing a section of driveway, sidewalk or floor without breaking up the rest
- Cutting openings for drains, sumps or footings in an existing floor
- Expansion and control joints

## What we need to quote

Where the job is, how thick the concrete is, and how many linear feet need to be cut. If you know what is under the slab (rebar, pipe, in-floor heat), tell us. If you do not, ask about scanning first.
