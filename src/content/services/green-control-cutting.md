## What it is

Green cutting means sawing control joints while the concrete is still "green," soon after it is poured. Control joints give the slab a planned place to crack as it cures and shrinks.

## When you need it

- New floors, slabs and driveways
- Commercial pours where joint layout is specified
- Any pour where random cracking is not acceptable

## What we need to quote

Pour date and time, square footage, slab thickness, and the joint layout or spacing you want.
