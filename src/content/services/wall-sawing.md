## What it is

Wall sawing uses a saw that runs on a track bolted to the wall, or a hand-held ring saw for smaller work, to cut openings in vertical concrete and block.

## When you need it

- Egress windows in a basement foundation
- New doorways or enlarging existing openings
- HVAC, plumbing and electrical penetrations through walls
- Cutting down footings and stem walls

## What we need to quote

Wall thickness, the size of the opening, and where the job is. Send a photo if you can. Let us know if you want the cut piece removed and hauled off.
